================================================================================
  SuperShadersSuiteRT — Credits
================================================================================

Universal post-processing, RT-lite lighting, and visual-preset addon for
UZDoom/GZDoom 4.10+ (hardware renderer).

Forum: https://forum.zdoom.org/viewtopic.php?t=62772

Legal terms and license texts: see LICENSE in this PK3.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Super Shaders Suite RT (this project)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  RENEGADE ANDROiD (Shawn Shackelford)     — lead maintenance and expansion (2026)
  Cynic Games                              — original suite foundation (2025)

  Original work in this PK3 includes, among other things:

    • Visual Presets (quality + style bundles across the whole stack)
    • RT-lite screen effects (Contact AO, Fluid SSR, wall-bake integration)
    • RT-Relight Enhance (map-load relight passes, tiered per preset)
    • Enhanced lighting pipeline (sector bias, smooth walls, fluid glow)
    • Door / lift light spill (runtime, lite)
    • Sprite floor / wall shadows (lite)
    • Planar fluid reflections
    • BodyCam Visual (digital bodycam shader + evidence overlay)
    • Menu integration, CVAR wiring, and preset orchestration (SSS* handlers)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Lighting & atmosphere
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DarkDoomZ
    Sterling "Caligari87" Parker
    Integrated sector darkness + flashlight (replaces standalone DarkDoomZ WAD)
    https://github.com/caligari87/darkdoomz

  Dark Doom (concept)
    SidDoyle / Josh771 — original Dark Doom mutator concept that DarkDoomZ
    reimplements in ZScript.

  ReLite (concepts & color-bleed grade)
    Hey Doomer
  ReLite is a fork of Relighting; this suite uses ReLite-inspired enhanced
  lighting ideas and adapts the color-bleed post grade (see Color Bleed below).
  Do not load the standalone ReLite or Relighting mods alongside this addon.

  Relighting (concepts)
    Nash Muhandes
  RT-Relight Enhance, recursive bleed, window/texture lights, door spill, and
  lite wall shadows are Relighting-inspired reimplementations — not a port of
  the standalone Relighting PK3.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GPU grading & tone mapping
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  MariFX / MariENB
    Marisa Kirisame (OrdinaryMagician)
    SHADERS/glsl/mfx_*.fp — luma sharpen, grain, dirt, grading, LUT grading,
    technicolor, color matrix, palette, retro FX, vignette, border blur, etc.
    https://github.com/OrdinaryMagician/marifx_m

  GIMP hue–saturation filter (inside mfx_huesaturation.fp)
    Michael Natterer (C)2007 — algorithm ported via MariENB

  ACES tonemap
    SHADERS/PostProcess/ACES.fp — camera-style exposure + ACES Narkowicz (fast)
    and full ACES (RRT+ODT) paths using standard filmic approximations.

  Filmic LUT tonemap
    kingeric1992 (ENB Series forum)
    SHADERS/FilmicTonemap.fp
    https://enbseries.enbdev.com/forum/viewtopic.php?f=7&t=4394

  Filmic pass (legacy variant)
    crosire — ReShade FilmicPass.fx (referenced in FilmicTonemap_old.fp)
    https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders

  SDPWH screen effects
    Filmic LUT path, chromatic aberration, and related post stack wired through
    SDPWHScreenEffects in zscript.zc (bundled with this suite).

  World gamma
    SHADERS/WorldGamma.fp — pre/post bloom gamma, contrast, brightness.

  Bloom boost
    SHADERS/BloomBoostPre.fp, BloomBoostPost.fp

  Retro display, CRT, VHS & screen effects

  Screen-M / Screem
    Widescreen pitch correction, phosphor / palette / CRT-style colour grading.
    SHADERS/Widescreem.fp, WidescreemZ.fp, OldPals.fp, Temp*.fp, Gamma.fp,
    Res.fp, Fos4.fp, Griller*.fp and ScreeMHandler in zscript.zc.

  VHS & CRT shader mod
    DRON12261
    SHADERS/PostProcess/VHSCRTShader.fp, VHSCRTPostProcessHandler.zs
    https://github.com/dron12261games/MOD-VHS-CRT-Shader-by-DRON12261

  VHS/CRT ShaderToy sources (credited upstream by DRON12261)
    Timothy Lottes   — FixingPixelArt (https://www.shadertoy.com/view/XsjSzR)
    Vladimir Storm   — VHS tape noise (https://www.shadertoy.com/view/MlfSWr)
    Gaktan           — VHS tape effect (https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Ms3XWH)

  SoftShade
    Ordered dither / palette-style reduction.
    SHADERS/PostProcess/SoftShade.fp, SoftShadePostProcessHandler.zs

  PSX Light (console gamma + 5:5:5 banding)
    Adapted from PSX DOOM CE by Molecicco (psxlight.fp only — material
    psx.fp lighting stack is not included).
    SHADERS/PostProcess/PSXLight.fp, Scripts/SSSPSXHandler.zs

  Old video / aged-film defects
    SHADERS/PostProcess/OldVideo.fp, OldVideoShaderHandler in zscript.zc

  Low-detail / resolution scaler
    SHADERS/Res.fp — code from the Low Detail Shader (community GZDoom shader)

  Screen-M temperature colour
    Tanner Helland — RGB temperature algorithm
    http://www.tannerhelland.com/4435/convert-temperature-rgb-algorithm-code/
    Used in SHADERS/Temp.fp, TempColour.fp, TempGrey.fp

  Screen-M saturation
    SHADERS/Gamma.fp — saturation approach adapted from The OpenGL Shading
    Language, 3rd ed., Chapter 16.

    Original bodycam.fp + SSSBodyCamHandler.zs for this suite.
